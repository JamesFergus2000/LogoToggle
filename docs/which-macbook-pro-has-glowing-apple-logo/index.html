Which MacBook Pro has the glowing Apple logo?

Only one: the MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display, which Apple released in November 2026. It comes in 14-inch and 16-inch sizes with the M5 Pro or M5 Max chip, and it's the Mac that LogoToggle is made for.

LogoToggle also needs macOS 27 or later.

How to check which MacBook Pro you have

  1. Choose Apple menu › About This Mac, then More Info.
  2. Look at the built-in display. If it's listed as a PROLED XDR Display, your Mac has the illuminated logo.
  3. Or just look at the lid: on this model the Apple logo glows whenever the Mac is awake, unless someone has turned it off.

Two other giveaways: the PROLED XDR model has a touch display, and its screens measure 14.2 and 16.3 inches.

Why only this model?

Older MacBooks lit the logo with light from the screen's backlight, and Apple dropped it in the mid-2010s. The PROLED XDR Display is OLED, so it has no backlight to borrow from. Instead, this MacBook Pro has a separate light behind the logo, which macOS dims and brightens with your screen brightness and the light in the room. That separate light is what LogoToggle switches.

Control the logo with LogoToggle

If you have the MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display, LogoToggle is a free, open-source menu bar app that turns the logo off or on in one click, and can make it breathe or strobe.

Install LogoToggle in one line

Paste into Terminal. It downloads the app, installs it into Applications and opens it.

curl -fsSL https://jamesfergus2000.github.io/LogoToggle/install.sh | sh

Frequently asked questions

Does the M6 MacBook Pro have a glowing Apple logo?

No. The 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6 has a Liquid Retina XDR display, and its logo doesn't light up. Only the MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display has the illuminated logo.

Does the MacBook Air have a glowing Apple logo?

No. No current MacBook Air has an illuminated logo.

Do both the 14-inch and 16-inch PROLED XDR models have it?

Yes. Both sizes of the MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display have the illuminated logo, with either the M5 Pro or the M5 Max chip.

Can I turn the glowing logo off?

Yes. Use Apple's Terminal command, or the free LogoToggle menu bar app, which does it in one click.
