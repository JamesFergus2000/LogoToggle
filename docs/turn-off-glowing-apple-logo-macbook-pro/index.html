How to turn off the glowing Apple logo on MacBook Pro

The new MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display has an illuminated Apple logo on the back of the lid. It's on by default, and there's no switch for it in System Settings. Here are two ways to turn it off, and how to turn it back on.

Method 1: Terminal (Apple's official way)

Apple's support article "About the illuminated Apple logo on MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display" documents a Terminal command. You'll need an administrator password.

  1. Open Terminal (Applications › Utilities, or search for it with Spotlight).
  2. Paste this command and press Return:
sudo defaults write com.apple.CoreBrightness LEBacklightMode -int 0
  1. Type your Mac password when asked (nothing appears as you type) and press Return.

If the logo doesn't change straight away, log out and back in.

Turn the logo back on

sudo defaults delete com.apple.CoreBrightness LEBacklightMode

Things to know

  • The setting applies to every user account on the Mac, not just yours.
  • Reinstalling macOS turns the logo back on.
  • There's no official way to set the logo's brightness. It dims and brightens automatically with your screen brightness and the ambient light in the room.

Method 2: LogoToggle (one click, from the menu bar)

If you'd rather not use Terminal every time, LogoToggle is a free, open-source menu bar app that does it in one click. It can also make the logo breathe with smooth fades, or strobe.

Install LogoToggle in one line

Paste into Terminal. It downloads the app, installs it into Applications and opens it.

curl -fsSL https://jamesfergus2000.github.io/LogoToggle/install.sh | sh

Frequently asked questions

Does turning off the logo save battery?

It uses a little power while lit, so turning it off saves a small amount. Don't expect a noticeable difference in battery life.

Does the logo turn off when the lid is closed or the Mac is asleep?

Yes. The logo follows the display, so it goes dark when the display sleeps or the lid is closed.

Which Macs have the glowing logo?

MacBook Pro models with the PROLED XDR Display, running macOS 27 or later.
